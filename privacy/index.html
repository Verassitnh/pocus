Pocus: Focus Timer — Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Pocus does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data storage

Your activities and time sessions are stored only on your device. The app never sends this data to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes the data.

No tracking, ads, or accounts

Pocus has no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no advertising, and no account or sign-in. It makes no network requests.

Children

Because no data is collected, Pocus is safe for users of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
